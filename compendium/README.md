## Osho
Prioritise primary discourse metadata, different periods in his teaching, active meditation as historical material, Zorba the Buddha, freedom, critical research on Rajneesh movement practices and leadership. Osho International Foundation asserts copyright and related rights over Osho's works; do not copy protected texts, videos, photos, music or trademarked methods without permission. Do not put strenuous breathwork or exhaustion-based cathartic practices in a casually accessible app.

## J. Krishnamurti
Prioritise Order of the Star dissolution in 1929, truth as a pathless land, the observer and observed, conditioning, choiceless awareness and relationship. Krishnamurti's rejection of teachers and fixed paths is the strongest corrective to the Oracle itself. User agency must prevail over any composite voice.

## Evidence and provenance contract
Every future content record should preserve source, author/editor/translator, edition, publication date, source URL, exact passage location when licensed, licensing status, download checksum, contemporary criticism, editorial review and claim status. Use documented historical source / historical claim / experimental investigation / independent verification / synthetic prompt. Never confuse existence of an archive with truth of its claims, and never automatically promote a claim's confidence. Do not copy modern translations, art or scans solely because they are available online.
