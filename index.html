A NOTE FROM NEURO//LAB

Use the colours as an invitation, not a verdict.

Cycle phases and timing are estimates based on the dates and averages you enter. They cannot confirm ovulation, explain every mood or symptom, or be relied on for contraception. You can track what you actually experience over time, and ignore anything that does not fit.

Your body is not a productivity schedule. Rest, energy, desire and mood can vary for many reasons.
